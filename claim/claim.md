Create your own iteration of the AVC program using your own choice of tools and host it on your own server.

Join the community and help spread Tokenmobiles's purpose by being a regular on our discord, post daily AVC updates of your specific Tokenmobiles on X/IG, create and share memes, defend the vision and share iterations created by the engineers. You shall recieve regular but limited time windows to claim Tokenmobiles. Sometimes TOP tier, sometimes mid tier.
