## TIER-1 - The Engineers

Create your own iteration of the AVC program using your own choice of tools and host it on your own server.
Test run your iteration on said server for a least 48 hours. if your iteration passes accuracy checks, you'll recieve a time window to claim 1st tier Tokenmobiles.
